# Coffee aur Code · learning material

A course of 38 lessons that teaches you to rebuild the *Psychology of Learning · Coffee aur Code* app from memory. Each lesson is a self-contained HTML page and works offline.

**Start here:** open [`index.html`](index.html) (the hub) in a browser. For a day-by-day schedule, open [`plan.html`](plan.html).

## How long it takes

Plan for about **78 hours** in total, or about **12–13 weeks** at the default pace.

| Part | Time |
|---|---|
| 38 lessons plus the mastery checkpoint | 48 h 55 min |
| 12 outside-reading items | 4 h |
| **New material** | **52 h 55 min** |
| Spaced reviews (each lesson comes back 4 times, for 10 min each: 152 reviews) | 25 h 20 min |
| **Total** | **78 h 15 min** |

### By level

| Level | Lessons | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 · Foundations (including 30 min of setup) | 5 | 6 h 05 min |
| Level 2 · Build it | 18 | 21 h 35 min |
| Level 3 · Under the hood | 6 | 7 h 05 min |
| Level 4 · Test it and ship it | 9 | 10 h 55 min |
| Mastery checkpoint | — | 3 h 15 min |

These are lesson minutes: study plus practice, with a ×1.3 buffer. The reading and review time comes on top.

### On the calendar

These finish dates assume you start on Monday 19 October 2026 and follow the plan's scheduling rules.

| Pace | Study days | You finish |
|---|---|---|
| 60 min/day, 5 days/week | 87 | 16 Feb 2027 |
| **90 min/day, 5 days/week (default)** | **62** | **12 Jan 2027** |
| 120 min/day, 5 days/week | 50 | 25 Dec 2026 |
| 120 min/day, 6 days/week | 50 | 15 Dec 2026 |
| 180 min/day, 7 days/week | 37 | 24 Nov 2026 |

Studying more per day stops helping after a point. The last lesson's final review always comes 14 study days after you finish it, so the plan can't go much below about 37 study days.

If you only read through the lessons once, without the reviews, the readings or the mastery checkpoint, it's about **45½ hours**. The reviews are what make it stick from memory.
